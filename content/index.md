# About Me

Enough about my site, what about me?

I spend a lot of my time developing, whether that is a web app, game mod, or entire game. Most of my development projects are private (although some may release in the future), but I have made things such as Discord Bots, Minecraft mods, and simplistic games. Despite it not being my preferred medium, most of what I release seems to end up being a web app. At some point, I want to try and change that, but I'll probably end up cancelling it halfway through again.

I have a wide variety of skills. I've made projects across Python, Java, Rust, TypeScript, lua and more, using tools such as [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz), (that's this site by the way) [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net), [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net), [Godot](https://godotengine.org) and [LÖVE](https://love2d.org).

Alongside programming, I also enjoy drawing and taking photos. I post a lot of drawings and photos as [Scribbles](/Scribbles). I usually prefer the editing side of photography, so a lot of the photos I produce are heavily edited.

Additionally, I play a lot of games. I stream these games on [my Twitch](https://to.1nteresting.name/twitch) and [my YouTube](https://to.1nteresting.name/yt), which is where I post edited videos of playthroughs. I mostly play Nintendo games, with Splatoon being my favourite series, Pikmin coming in close second. My favourite game, however, is Minecraft, which I would estimate I have nearly a whole year of playtime in. I'm also a fan of platformers, RPGs and Rhythm games, such as [Beat Saber](https://to.1nteresting.name/bs) and [osu!](https://to.1nteresting.name/osu) I also like to [speedrun](https://to.1nteresting.name/sp) some games.
