Just a few days ago, I managed to reach S Rank in Splatoon 3. Sucks that the season will end in a few days and I'll be back to A, but to celebrate, I drew this image of my Octoling in the Lobby celebrating next to the rank-up message. I have done this pose before in my [Splatoon Splatoween Spooky Scribble](/Scribbles/Splatoon-Splatoween-Spooky-Scribble), but the outfit and specific angle are a little different, so I think it's fine. When I get better at drawing stuff with less accurate references, I'll try some more poses.

Speaking of less accurate references, I drew this using the background of the rank-up screen, which is blurred, as my main reference, and used a screenshot of the lobby from a different position to fill in the details. This was basically just to challenge myself to see if I could do better with a worse reference, and I think it turned out quite well. I'll probably try a similar challenge for whatever my next Scribble is.

Anyway, I have nothing much left to speak about as I have to prepare to grind all the way back to S rank from A next season, so just enjoy these alternate versions, one missing the results screen, and one that is just the background.
