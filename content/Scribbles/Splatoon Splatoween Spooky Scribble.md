It's October, and you know what that means! Halloween is here, and the first thing everyone thinks about when they think of Halloween is Splatoon 3, of course! This weekend, Splatoween is upon us, and to celebrate, I've done a very spooky scribble for two stream thumbnails. There's one for Day 1, and one for Day 2.

These are both based on poses I took in-game using Photo Mode, and let me tell you, getting the Enchanted Hat was so annoying. I realise now I could have created Splatoon 2 Save Data on an Octoling Girl amiibo and then scanning it in Splatoon 3, but instead I decided to go the harder route, which requires winning 20 matches after scanning the amiibo. Coincidentally, while grinding for this item, I had my longest streak of losses I can remember. Additionally, every 30 or so minutes, my internet would completely drop out, it would be back 2 minutes later, I'd get disconnect banned, and I would have to wait ages to keep playing. 

With that aside, the poses are based on the reference images below, with a background sourced from [Inkipedia](https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/File:S3_Splatoween_Costumed_Snake.png) of the snake in Splatsville. This was taken in Zombie vs Skeleton vs Ghost, but supposedly that fest had the exact same ink colours as the upcoming rerun of Wizard vs Knight vs Ninja.
